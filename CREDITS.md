Original game logic, interface, procedural sound, and Blender assets were created for this repository. Fictional characters and events do not depict actual school staff or students.

- **OpenStreetMap contributors** — school, streets, paths, and surrounding building footprints; Open Database License (ODbL). [Attribution and license](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Derived source data is retained with the Blender generator. Geometry and height tags are community map data and do not constitute a verified building survey.

- **Don McCurdy** — [three-gltf-viewer](https://github.com/donmccurdy/three-gltf-viewer), inspected for glTF loading, lighting, and tone mapping. Reference commit `bd83b39339a525708387006aba976342f036713e`. MIT. No viewer source was copied into the game.
